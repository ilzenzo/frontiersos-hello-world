04 / Super App for customers

Know them.
Follow them.
Guide them.

The ambition: move beyond a portal customers must navigate, towards a companion that helps them move forward.

Not another destination. Relevant help within the Publishing Platform, where the customer is already working.

01

Know who they are.

Recognise the signed-in customer, their role, and the manuscripts or Research Topics they can access. Use that context so they do not have to start from zero.

02

Stay with their journey.

Carry relevant context as they move between publishing steps. Follow their progress within Frontiers—not their activity elsewhere—so help stays connected to the task at hand.

03

Make the next step clear.

Explain what needs attention, guide permitted actions, and connect them to a person when needed. Help an author understand a revision, a reviewer find their next task, or a Topic Editor manage a collection.

Product vision. Customer features will roll out in stages, subject to identity, permissions, and connected publishing services.

07 / From vision to evidence

Start focused.
Prove value. Scale.

Proposed first pilot: the Super App within Research Topic Management. A defined customer group, a narrow set of tasks, and a clear path to human support.

01 / ENABLE

Connect the essentials.

Make the first customer journey ready for a controlled pilot.

  • Research Topic read and update services.
  • Publishing Platform identity and role-based permissions.
  • Action boundaries, audit trail, and human handoff.
02 / PILOT

Help with real tasks.

Embed the Super App where Topic Editors already work.

  • Explain their Research Topic’s context.
  • Guide a small set of permitted next steps.
  • Compare completion, effort, and support demand with a baseline.
03 / LEARN & SCALE

Expand on evidence.

Improve the experience before extending its scope.

  • Review quality, customer feedback, and cost.
  • Resolve failure points and refine guidance.
  • Consider article support once its services and permissions are ready.
Today: FrontonGPT is the employee experience on FrontiersOS. Next: establish customer-service readiness and run a focused Super App pilot. Vision: a connected companion across the publishing journey.

Proposed sequence, not a committed launch schedule. Customer readiness, scope, resources, and timing must be confirmed before kickoff.

08 / What success looks like

Measure the progress.
Not just the conversations.

Use business outcomes across both experiences. For the first customer pilot, focus on customer task completion, staff support effort, quality, and cost.

For employees

  • Time spent on routine tasks.
  • Effort needed to resolve support requests.
  • Capacity available for researcher support.

For customers

  • Successful completion of the selected tasks.
  • Time to resolution and repeat requests.
  • Satisfaction with the help received.

For the business

  • Cost per successfully completed task.
  • Answer quality and action accuracy.
  • Capabilities reused in the next journey.

Set the baseline before launch. Agree the target outcomes, comparison method, and review window. Track failures and human handoffs—not just successful interactions. Expand only when the pilot meets the agreed value and quality thresholds, with permissions enforced and actions auditable.
